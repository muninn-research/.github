# Muninn Research

Research group on **energy-autonomous embedded systems** and **sustainable IoT**, based at the Department of Industrial Engineering, University of Trento, Italy.

We design devices that sense, compute and communicate with as little energy as possible, and often with none from a battery.

## Why Muninn?

In Norse mythology, Odin's two ravens, Huginn ("thought") and Muninn ("memory"), fly across the world every dawn and return to report what they have seen. Odin fears for both, but more for Muninn.

Our devices do the same: they venture into the environment, sense it on almost no energy, and must never lose their memory when the power goes out. That is the core challenge of intermittent computing.

## Research areas

- **Energy harvesting & batteryless devices**: systems powered by their environment
- **Intermittent computing**: software that survives power failures
- **Green TinyML & on-device intelligence**: machine learning on resource-constrained platforms
- **Ultra-low-power hardware–software co-design**
- **Nature-integrated sensing systems**
- **Ultra-wideband (UWB) localization**
- **Flexible and printed electronics**
